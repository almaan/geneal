geneal — risk-aware target nomination

3 cell lines · 2 seeds · K=30 targets/portfolio
Efficacy-only nomination puts 57% of the portfolio in one pathway and carries toxicity 0.74. The selective+hedged strategy (selective_cap2) cuts toxicity to 0.67 and concentration to 11%, while keeping max-efficacy 2.84 vs 2.89 (mean efficacy 1.48→1.02).

How to read this

Strategies
• efficacy — pick the K targets with highest predicted lethality, ignoring toxicity and pathway. The naive baseline (“maximize efficacy, check safety later”).
• selective — pick K maximizing lethality MINUS a common-essential toxicity penalty (joint efficacy+safety optimization).
• selective_cap<c> — selective, but allow at most c genes per pathway/complex (e.g. cap2 = max 2 per pathway). The portfolio hedge against pathway-level toxicity/failure.

Why hedge? If you nominate many targets in one pathway and that pathway proves toxic in normal tissue (or undruggable), the whole bet fails together. Capping spreads the bet across independent mechanisms.
